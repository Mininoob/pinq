Brand colors
------------
Sticky yellow: #FFD45C
Coral fold:    #FF6F6C
Ink:           #242322
Warm cream:    #FFF8EE

Usage
-----
Use pinq-app-icon-1024.png for app listings. Do not add rounded corners; iOS and
Android apply their own masks. Use the transparent SVG or PNG for websites,
social profiles, and in-app surfaces.
